Native mod by Lutziano888

Fair Freecam

Fly the camera freely while your player stays where it is, with an optional fair play mode.

No ratings yet·692 downloads·Version 1.2.0·MIT

Made by Lutziano888. Its authors own it and answer for it. Not made or endorsed by the Horizon project. Mod policy

Press F4 to leave your body and fly the camera anywhere. Your player stays where it is and you see it from outside. Press F4 again to snap back.

Controls while flying:

  • W A S D: fly where you look
  • Space / Shift: up / down
  • Ctrl: 2.5x faster
  • Mouse wheel: change the speed

Settings (Options > Mods > Fair Freecam):

  • Speed: 2, 5, 10, 20, 40 or 80 blocks per second.
  • Fair play: the camera can't fly through blocks, so no peeking into caves or bases through walls. Glass, fences and doors stop it, grass and flowers don't.
  • Allowed on servers: turn it off to only use freecam in your own worlds.

While flying, your player doesn't move, attack, mine or use items.

Some servers don't allow freecam, so check their rules first.

Native code

A native mod is Rust code that the launcher compiles into a game of its own, the Native mods instance. It is not sandboxed: it can do anything you can on this computer. The launcher builds it only after a warning that names it, and a reviewer read its source before it was listed. Read the source yourself before you build it. How native mods work

From a terminal or an agent

horizon-launcher mods install --source horizon fair_freecam

In the launcher

Open Mods, pick Horizon beside Modrinth, find Fair Freecam and press Install. It checks the file's checksum first.

By hand

Download fair_freecam-1.2.0.hnmod with the button above, check its SHA-256 against the one under File, and unzip it into a folder, then build the game with it: horizon-launcher native-build FOLDER --trust-native-code. It plays in the Native mods instance.
